Quality control

How an engineer reaches your team

The whole model rests on who we put in front of you, so nothing skips these four stages.

1

Screen

Stack match, seniority, written and spoken English, and enough timezone overlap to work in your stand-ups.

2

Assess

Live coding and a system design conversation with one of our senior engineers — not a take-home puzzle that proves nothing.

3

Pressure-test

How they explain trade-offs, handle disagreement and ask for help. Most failed placements fail here, not on syntax.

4

Your call

You interview every candidate and make the final decision. We never place an engineer you haven’t met yourself.

If it still isn’t right

A two-week replacement window, at no cost

Vetting reduces the risk of a bad match but it can’t remove it — sometimes fit only shows up in real work. Tell us within the first two weeks and we start a replacement search immediately, with proper handover from the outgoing engineer, so your team doesn’t absorb the cost of our mistake.
