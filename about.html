Our story

Two practices, because growth stalls in two different places

Every venture we met was stuck on one of two things. Either they couldn't build fast enough — hiring took months, ops ran on spreadsheets, the cloud migration kept slipping. Or they could build fine, but nobody knew who they were.

So we built for both. A tech practice that adds engineers, AI automation and cloud capability directly into your team. A non-tech practice that builds the partnerships, brand, campaigns and platforms that bring customers in. One contract, one point of contact, either or both.

Our process

How an engagement actually runs

The same four stages whether you're engaging the tech practice, the non-tech practice, or both.

1

Scope

A 30-minute call, then a written scope of the gap — headcount, process, pipeline or platform.

2

Match

Engineer profiles, a technical plan or a growth strategy — with timelines and costs attached.

3

Embed

Our people join your tools, stand-ups and channels. Delivery starts inside two weeks.

4

Scale

Fortnightly reviews, and the engagement flexes up or down as your roadmap changes.
