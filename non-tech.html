Search is splitting in three

Ranking on Google is no longer the whole job

A growing share of searches never produce a click. The buyer asks an AI assistant and reads a synthesised answer instead. If your business isn't structured to be cited in that answer, you're invisible to them — however well you rank on page one.

  • SEO — technical health, content and links for classic rankings
  • AEO — structured answers for snippets, People Also Ask and voice
  • GEO — entity and citation work so AI tools quote you by name
  • Schema markup and clean data feeding all three

Common questions

SEO earns you rankings in the traditional list of blue links. AEO — answer engine optimisation — targets the direct answer slots: featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice results. GEO — generative engine optimisation — is about being cited inside AI-generated answers in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews. They share foundations but need different content structures, so we run all three together.

Two routes. Partnerships open a channel through companies who already serve your buyers, which compounds over time. Direct lead generation builds a target account list and runs multichannel outreach from your brand, booking qualified meetings into your calendar.

All three sell on trust and perception rather than feature lists, and all three carry constraints most agencies get wrong — regulatory language in healthcare, restraint in luxury, consistency across every physical touchpoint in hospitality. Depth in a few categories produces better work than being generic across all of them.
